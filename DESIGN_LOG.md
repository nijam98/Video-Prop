# Design log

## Rumah teres 2 tingkat, dekat Sungai Petani — "Galeri Linen" (Nov 2026)
- Source: phone walk-through, 576x1024, 104.7 s, voiceover, no face. Cut to 53.6 s + 2.4 s end card (56 s).
- Project: `rumah-luxe/` (HyperFrames). Cut: `tools/edl.json` + `tools/cut.py`; captions: `tools/captions.py`; shared styles: `tools/common.css/js` + `tools/build.py`.
- Look: quiet-luxury editorial / gallery. Ivory #F5F0E8, espresso #1C1713, taupe #A39382, one oxblood accent #7B2D26.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond Light + Italic (titles, keywords), Manrope (tracked uppercase kickers, captions).
- Signature: room titles that blur in with a scale settle under a hairline kicker; frosted ivory placards with an inset hairline frame;
  photo prints with a white mat and a shutter flash; dark "page" with a hairline mat for location and the end card; oxblood "Unit terhad" seal.
- Captions: Manrope 40 px ivory, keyword swapped to Cormorant italic, lower band inside the TikTok/Reels safe area (x 120-780, bottom <= 1248).
- Transitions: ivory flash on the hook's flash-forward cuts, ivory veil into the tour, espresso curtain for "Tingkat Atas", page rise for the ending; alternating 1.0/1.045 framing on jump cuts.
- Hook: flash-forward montage (living, master, kitchen) under "Cantik tak cantik? Sila komen." with "Mampu milik, rasa mewah." on frame 0.
- Music: "Hazy After Hours" — Alejandro Magaña (Mixkit, free licence), 121 BPM, entered on a bar at 63.5 s so the track's own outro ends the video.
- SFX (Mixkit): soft wind swooshes, camera shutters on photo prints, one deep impact on the hook and the seal.
- Cut out: "ataupun bilik tetamu pun" repeat, second "ruangan atas", "ha bilik kedua", "petak juga", "fully tiled" repeats, "alright macam-macam lah", "bye-bye", every "okey/alright" lead-in, pauses > 0.3 s.

Next video: avoid ivory/espresso/oxblood, Cormorant + Manrope, matted photo prints, hairline kickers and the dark end page.
